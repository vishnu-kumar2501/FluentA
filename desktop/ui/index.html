Privacy & Data Transparency

🛡️

Privacy by Architecture

FluentA is built on the principle that your writing belongs solely to you. We do not track, monetize, or train AI on your text.

✓ Zero Keylogging ✓ No AI Training ✓ Air-Gapped Offline Capable ✓ Zero Telemetry

1. Zero Keylogging & Password Safety

FluentA is not a keylogger. It never records raw keystrokes or background typing. It only inspects text when you trigger checking or pause typing in a supported text field. All password, PIN, and sensitive credential fields are strictly ignored by Windows UI Automation classification.

2. Zero AI Model Training

Your writing, grammar corrections, and style choices are never used to train, retrain, or fine-tune public or proprietary AI models. Text is processed in-memory for inference and discarded immediately.

3. 100% Offline Air-Gapped Mode (Local Ollama)

If you have strict data sovereignty or enterprise compliance needs, switch to Local Ollama in Preferences. When selected, 100% of grammar and style inference runs locally on your PC. Zero bytes of text ever leave your computer.

4. Encrypted Cloud AI Transit

When using Cloud AI (Groq / OpenAI API), text is transmitted exclusively over an encrypted TLS 1.3 HTTPS connection, held ephemerally in server RAM solely to generate the suggestion, and immediately discarded.

5. Excluded Applications Blocklist

You can block sensitive apps (e.g. 1password.exe, keepass.exe, cmd.exe) in Preferences. FluentA instantly deactivates itself whenever any excluded application is focused.

6. Local Data Control & Instant Wipe

Your revision history is stored strictly on your local machine at %APPDATA%\FluentA\history.json. You can wipe your entire history at any time with one click in the History tab. Uninstalling FluentA removes all data completely.
